Support

Help with CineTrack.

Most answers are below. If yours isn't, write to hello@cinetrackapp.com. I make CineTrack on my own, so a reply can take a day or two, and a little longer in the week of the ceremony.

Before launch

How do I get CineTrack?

I'm hoping to have CineTrack 3 on the App Store by the end of the year. Leave your email with the form, which writes a short email for you to send, and I'll let you know the day it's out, and now and then after that if there's news. You can ask me to stop at any time.

Can I test it before then?

Testing is open to people with an iPhone Duo for now. Tick the box in the same form, giving the email address on your Apple Account. If there's a spot, an invite to Apple's TestFlight arrives by email: install the free TestFlight app, open the invite and tap Install.

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 27 or iPadOS 27 or later. To check, open Settings → General → About. It won't install on anything older.

What does it cost?

CineTrack is free, with no ads. An optional Season Pass, a one-time purchase and not a subscription, will add live results on ceremony night. I haven't set its price yet. The winners and final scores are free for everyone.

Is a beta safe to use?

It's the same app that will go to the App Store, but it can still have bugs. Your films and ratings sync to your own iCloud, which protects them if something goes wrong on one device. To send feedback, take a screenshot in the app and choose Share Beta Feedback, or write to the address above.

Getting started

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 27 or iPadOS 27 or later, including iPhone Duo.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. To play in a pool you choose a display name, and that is all.

How do I switch seasons?

Open the Films tab and tap the season name at the top. Past seasons keep everything you marked.

Films

A film I watched is not in the list.

Tap Add a Film, search for it and add it to the season. If it is later shortlisted or nominated, it keeps your rating and watch date.

The list of films has not updated.

When shortlists or nominations are announced, a banner appears at the top of the Films tab. Tap it to download the new list. The update can take a little while to appear after an announcement, because the list is checked by hand.

Where to Watch shows nothing, or looks wrong.

Streaming availability comes from JustWatch and is shown for the United States. Services change their catalogues often, so it can lag behind.

How does Letterboxd work?

In Settings, enter your Letterboxd username. CineTrack reads your public diary and marks those films as watched, with your rating and date where you have not set them here. It never changes anything on Letterboxd, and it never overwrites a rating you gave in CineTrack. Letterboxd shares your 50 most recent diary entries, and your diary has to be public.

Sync

My progress is not on my other device.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on. Then open Settings in CineTrack, tap Send Progress on the device that is up to date and Receive Progress on the other.

Ballot and ceremony night

Can I change my picks?

Yes, as often as you like until the ceremony begins. Then the ballot locks.

How do I get the live score on my Lock Screen?

Open CineTrack within an hour of the ceremony starting, and the Live Activity begins on its own. Live Activities have to be allowed for CineTrack in the Settings app.

A winner is late or wrong.

Winners are entered by hand during the show, usually within a minute or so. If something looks wrong, I'll correct it and your score will follow.

Pools

How do I invite friends?

Create a pool in the Pools tab, then share its code or QR code. Your friends enter the code in their own Pools tab.

Why can't I create or join a pool?

Pools open when nominations are announced and close when the ceremony begins. The host can also lock a pool earlier.

When can others see my picks?

Only once the ceremony starts. Before that, members can see how much of your ballot is filled in, and nothing else.

How do I leave or delete a pool?

Open the pool and use the menu at the top. Leaving removes you from it. Deleting, which only the host can do, removes it for everyone.

I want my pool entries removed.

Email the address above with the pool's name or code and your display name. See the privacy policy for the details.

Reporting a problem

Write to hello@cinetrackapp.com and say which device you have, your iOS version and the CineTrack version shown in Settings. A screenshot helps.

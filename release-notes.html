Release notes

What's new in CineTrack.

Version 3.0

November 2026

A new CineTrack for the 2027 season, with a new design, layouts for iPad and iPhone Duo, and a better ceremony night.

  • A welcome tour. A few short steps on what the app does, where the season stands and which films you have already seen. It only adds, so nothing you have marked is changed, and you can replay it from Settings.
  • A new look. A new icon, and a split-flap board for every countdown, the live score and your share images.
  • Four tabs. Films, Ballot, Pools and Stats, each one tap away, with the season menu on every one.
  • Made for big screens. On iPad and an open iPhone Duo, lists sit beside the film, category or pool you have open. Fold the Duo part-way on ceremony night and the score moves to its own half of the screen.
  • Add any film. Search for a film and add it to a season before the shortlists arrive. If it is nominated later, it keeps your rating. The sheet suggests films from the season, and a film goes to the season of its US cinema release, not its festival premiere.
  • A Show filter on Films. Switch between All Films, Nominees, Shortlisted, My Additions and Winners, each with a count.
  • Where a film stands. A film's page says under the title whether it was shortlisted or nominated, and what it won.
  • Letterboxd. Connect your username and the films in your diary are marked as watched, with your ratings.
  • Watch dates. Record the day you watched each film, and sort by it.
  • Your Year and a watch calendar in Stats, with a month or the whole year you can share as an image.
  • Milestones that fit the season. Before nominations they count the films you have watched; afterwards, your share of the nominees.
  • Ceremony reminders. Turn them on in Settings, or when you make your first pick, for a reminder the day before the ceremony and another an hour before your ballot locks.
  • A new Live Activity. A countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island in the hour before the ceremony, your score and the latest winner during the show, and your final score at the end.
  • Better sync. Progress, ballot picks and nomination predictions merge between devices and show up without a relaunch.
  • Private pools. A pool can now be read only by its members.
  • No calendar permission. Add to Calendar opens a new event without asking for access to your calendar.
  • A faster first launch, and many smaller fixes.

Version 2.0

January to March 2026

  • Multi-select. Mark several films as watched and rate them in one go.
  • Ceremony night. A live dashboard, streaks, celebrations for correct picks and a results card to share.
  • Up Next. A watch queue with quick ways to fill it.
  • Nominations and predictions. Guess the nominees from the shortlists, then see how you did.
  • Awards pools. Compete with friends on ceremony night.

Version 1.0

2024 and 2025

Where it started: a list of the nominated films, a way to tick them off, and a ballot.

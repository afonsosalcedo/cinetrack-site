Release notes

What's new in CineTrack.

Version 3.0, build 20

21 November 2026

The first update to the beta: reminders that work, and a Live Activity rebuilt for ceremony night.

  • Ceremony reminders. Turn them on in Settings, or when you make your first pick, for a reminder the day before the ceremony and another an hour before your ballot locks. Earlier builds could not send them at all.
  • A new Live Activity. The countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island now ticks on its own in the hour before the ceremony. During the show it keeps your score and the latest winner in the app's split-flap style, and it ends with your final score.
  • The season in every tab. Switch seasons from Ballot, Pools and Stats as well as Films.
  • One countdown everywhere. The nominations card in Stats and the date in Pools use the same flap board as the ceremony countdown.
  • Nominations waits for the shortlists. Before a season has shortlists, the Nominations tab counts down to them instead of showing empty categories.
  • Add to Calendar without the permission prompt. CineTrack no longer asks for access to your calendar.
  • Fixes. A switch of season on ceremony night could score your ballot against the wrong year's winners. Best Director picks made on a brand-new ballot could be marked wrong. Two leftover placeholder widgets are gone.

Version 3.0, build 19

21 November 2026

A new CineTrack for the 2027 season: redesigned from the icon in, and at home on every screen.

  • A new look. One gold accent, calm cards and a split-flap board for the countdown, the live score and your share images. A new icon to match.
  • Four tabs. Films, Ballot, Pools and Stats, each one tap away.
  • Made for big screens. On iPad and an open iPhone Duo, lists sit beside the film, category or pool you have open. Fold the Duo part-way on ceremony night and the score moves to its own half of the screen.
  • Add any film. Search for a film and add it to a season before the shortlists arrive. If it is nominated later, it keeps your rating.
  • Letterboxd. Connect your username and the films in your diary are marked as watched, with your ratings.
  • Watch dates. Record the day you watched each film, and sort by it.
  • Your Year and a watch calendar in Stats, with a month you can share as an image.
  • Better sync. Progress, ballot picks and nomination predictions merge between devices and show up without a relaunch.
  • Private pools. A pool can now be read only by its members.
  • A faster first launch, and many smaller fixes.

Version 2.0

January to March 2026

  • Multi-select. Mark several films as watched and rate them in one go.
  • Ceremony night. A live dashboard, streaks, celebrations for correct picks and a results card to share.
  • Up Next. A watch queue with quick ways to fill it.
  • Nominations and predictions. Guess the nominees from the shortlists, then see how you did.
  • Awards pools. Compete with friends on ceremony night.

2024 and 2025

Where it started: a list of the nominated films, a way to tick them off, and a ballot.
